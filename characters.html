Meet the Characters

The heroes (and not-so-heroes) of HDBOC comics.

❓ Which character are YOU? Take the quiz! 🖍️ Make your own comic 🃏 Collect trading cards

From Not Smart Cops

Barry

Cop. Not smart.

The tall one with the mustache. Claims to be one of "Paris's Finest." Once lost a fight to a mosquito.

Lou

Barry's partner. Also not smart.

Says he's "a trained professional." His mosquito-swatting skills say otherwise. In charge of the loot bag.

From PrayingAntis

PrayingAntis

The buggiest hero in the city.

A praying mantis and an ant who got melted together by toxic waste. Mantis body, ant head, human-sized. Anything he bites catches fire!

Biscuit

The lab dog... for now.

The bug lab's sleepy dog. Got splashed by the toxic waste too. That's never a good sign...

From Not Smart Sports: Baseball Edition

Gus

Wants to win the World Series.

Not on a baseball team. Didn't let that stop him. Hits home runs (and eats bagels).

Ray

Gus's best friend. Bagel carrier.

Never goes anywhere without bagels from Mo's. Got them both jerseys just by asking nicely.

From The Heroes with Twisted Legs

Max

The clumsiest kid in the world.

Trips over EVERYTHING. Now his legs are twisted together with Toby's... forever. He says it's not SO bad.

Toby

Max's best friend. Ideas guy.

Had the slippery butter idea to get untangled. It did NOT work. Still saved the world by falling off a building.

Dr. Blackout

Turned off the WHOLE world.

Built the BLACKOUT-O-MATIC and wanted $1,000,000,000,000. Got Max and Toby on his head instead. Now he's in jail.

From Praying Antis 2

Pooant

The newest hero in town!

An ant with a yellow cape and a POO blaster. His poo washes the evil right out of you. (Smells bad, though.)

Baron Von Bolt

The one who sent the robot.

Jetpack, goggles and a laser arm he can turn up to MAXIMUM. He won the big fight... for now.

Battle Bot

Built by B.V.B.

A giant robot with a drill, a claw and tank treads. PrayingAntis already broke it once. The clue on it said B.V.B...
