✈️ Flying Tips

Got an HDBOC plane? Here's how to make it fly its best!

For every plane

  • Hold it underneath, near the middle, between your thumb and finger.
  • Keep the wings even. If one wing is higher, gently fix it before you throw.
  • Nose dives down? Bend the back edges of the wings up a tiny bit.
  • Swoops up and stalls? Bend the back edges down a tiny bit.
  • Turns to one side? Bend the back edge of one wing the other way to steer it.
  • Find a big space like a gym, a hallway, or outside with no wind.
  • Never throw at people or faces. Planes have pointy noses!

The Original

🎯 Throw: straight ahead, medium speed

Aim a little bit up and give it a smooth push forward, like throwing a dart. Don't throw it too hard or it will dive.

Looper

🔄 Throw: upside down and underhand!

Flip the Looper over so it's upside down, then throw it underhand (swing your arm up from below, like tossing a ball softly). That's the secret that makes it do its loop-the-loops!

Sky Fin

🪽 Throw: hard and a little up

The fins keep it flying straight, so you can throw it harder than the others. Aim slightly up and let it glide. Best in a big space where it can go far!

Sky Fin+

⚡ Throw: fast and level

This one is built for speed. Throw it quick and straight, with the nose pointing level. It slices through the air, so it's great for distance contests!
