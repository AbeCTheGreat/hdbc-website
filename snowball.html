❄️ Snowball Fight!

Barry and Lou are hiding behind the snow forts. When they pop up, click or tap them to throw a snowball! Be quick, or they'll throw one back at you. Hit Biscuit 🐶 for 3 points!

❤️ You have 3 hearts. If a snowball is coming at you, click it to block it! Every 3 points = 1 coin for the Paper Airplane Game.
