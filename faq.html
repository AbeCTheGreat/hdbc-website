❓ Questions? We've Got Answers

Click a question to see the answer.

🛒 How do I order?

Go to the Order page. Type how many of each thing you want, pick a pickup day, and click Order these. Then fill in your name, classroom, and age on the form and click Submit.

💵 How do I pay?

Pay cash when you get your order. You don't pay anything online.

📍 Where and when do I pick up my order?

After school at the playground, by the spider web. Pickups are on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. You pick the day when you order.

📅 How soon can I get my order?

The earliest pickup day is tomorrow (if it's a Monday, Wednesday, or Friday). That gives us time to fold your planes and get your comics ready.

💰 Are there any deals?

Yes! The Comic Combo is all 3 comics for $12 (save $2), and the Plane Combo is any comic + any 2 paper airplanes for $5. See the deals.

🎟️ What's the punch card?

Ask for a punch card at pickup! Every time you buy a paper airplane, we punch a hole. After 5 planes, your 6th plane is FREE.

✈️ My plane doesn't fly well. What do I do?

Check out our Flying Tips! Each plane has its own best way to throw. (Hint: the Looper goes upside down and underhand.)

🔧 What if my plane breaks?

Paper planes can get bent or crumpled. Bring it to pickup and we'll try to fix it for you.

📚 Can I read the comics before I buy?

Yes! Read page 1 of every comic for free on our Sneak Peeks page.

🎮 Can I spend my game coins on real stuff?

Nope, sorry! Game coins are only for hats and skins in the Paper Airplane Game. Real planes and comics cost real money.

✋ Who makes everything?

Everything is made by hand by HDBOC Paper Products. Every comic is written and drawn by hand, and every plane is folded by hand.
